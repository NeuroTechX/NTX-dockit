Starting a Student Club
=======================

A guide for students starting a university neurotechnology club, and for existing clubs joining the NeuroTechX network.

Welcome
-------

A NeuroTechX student club is a student-run group that learns and builds neurotechnology together. The clubs already in the network are listed on `neurotechx.org/students <https://neurotechx.org/students>`__.

Your club is independent. It has its own officers and follows its own university's rules. NeuroTechX has no authority over it. What we offer is a network: other clubs to learn from, visibility, and a name your university can recognise.

**A club or a chapter?** A club belongs to one university and its members are students. A chapter belongs to a city and is open to everyone. If your city has a chapter, work with it. There is a separate `guide for chapters <https://neurotechx.org/community/start-a-chapter>`__.

What you get
------------

* **A listing** on `neurotechx.org/students <https://neurotechx.org/students>`__ with your website and contact address.
* **An introduction in the NeuroTechX Slack**, where the other clubs' officers are.
* **A support letter** for your application to become an official student organization, for a funding request at your university, or for a sponsor.
* **Hackathon support**: help planning and promoting a hackathon you run, and help with mentors, judges, prizes and devices.
* **Headset loans**: EEG headsets on loan for a workshop or a hackathon, on request.
* **Global NeuroHack 2027**: an invitation for your club to take part.
* **Open-source projects to start from**: `EEG-ExPy <https://github.com/NeuroTechX/EEG-ExPy>`__, `MOABB <https://github.com/NeuroTechX/moabb>`__, `awesome-bci <https://github.com/NeuroTechX/awesome-bci>`__ and the `Neurotech Primer <https://neurotechx.org/primer>`__.
* **Your events** on `neurotechx.org/events <https://neurotechx.org/events>`__ and in the newsletter when you send them to us.
* **Jobs and internships** at neurotech companies, on `neurotechx.org/jobs <https://neurotechx.org/jobs>`__.

What we cannot offer today: a budget from NeuroTechX's own funds, devices to keep, or an annual competition. The last student competition was 2021–2022. We will announce it if one returns.

What we ask
-----------

#. **Hold a first meeting.** That is what starts a club. After that the schedule is yours: set it around your own academic year.
#. **Follow the** `Code of Conduct <https://neurotechx.org/code-of-conduct>`__, alongside your university's own rules.
#. **Use a club address, not a personal one**, and tell us each year who the new officers are. Clubs disappear when the founder graduates and nobody else has the passwords.

Starting a club
---------------

#. **Find your people and hold a first meeting.** Three committed students is enough to start. Look for a mentor too: a graduate student or a professor in neuroscience, engineering or computer science.
#. **Organise.** Agree who does what, set up a shared drive, and pick a name. Use your university's name, such as "Neurotech at McGill". "NeuroTechX" followed by a city is the form for city chapters.
#. **Get recognised by your university.** Official student-organization status is what unlocks meeting rooms, funding and a place at the activities fair. Being able to point to the clubs already in the NeuroTechX network helps the application. Ask us for a support letter.
#. **Put up a web page.** One page is enough: who you are, your university, what you are building, how to join, how to contact you.
#. **Build skills before building products.** Start by reproducing a known experiment: an SSVEP speller, a P300 demo, an EMG controller. EEG-ExPy has these ready to run.
#. **Run something public.** An intro night, a "Neurotech 101" workshop, a demo table. This is how next year's members find you.
#. **Tell us.** Write to `studentclubs@neurotechx.org <mailto:studentclubs@neurotechx.org>`__ with your club name, university, web page and contact address. We list you and introduce you in Slack.

Already running a club?
-----------------------

Send the same four things to `studentclubs@neurotechx.org <mailto:studentclubs@neurotechx.org>`__: club name, university, web page, contact address. If you were on the old NeuroTechX club list, tell us, so we can bring your entry up to date.

Your first year
---------------

* **Month 1:** first meeting; a core team of three, a mentor, a name, a shared drive.
* **Month 2:** application for student-organization status; a one-page website.
* **Months 2–3:** first public event, such as an intro night with a live demo.
* **Months 3–6:** weekly or fortnightly build sessions; reproduce one known experiment.
* **Months 6–9:** one project of your own, in a public repository.
* **Before the summer:** elect next year's officers, hand over the accounts, and send us the new contact.

Handing over
------------

Most clubs that vanish do so at a handover. Before the officers graduate:

* Next year's officers are chosen and have met the mentor.
* The club mailbox, website, repository and social accounts each have two current administrators.
* A one-page note says what the club ran this year and what it owns.
* NeuroTechX has the new contact.

Money and the name
------------------

* Your club's own money is handled under your university's rules for student organizations.
* **A sponsor who would rather pay a registered non-profit can pay NeuroTechX**, which issues the invoice and the receipt. Write to us before you agree it. NeuroTechX then either pays the vendor directly or repays an officer against receipts.
* Do not sign an agreement in NeuroTechX's name yourselves.
* NeuroTechX is not a registered charity. Its receipts are business receipts, not charitable-donation tax receipts.
* You may say your club is "part of the NeuroTechX student club network" and use the logo from the `brand kit <https://github.com/NeuroTechX/Resource-Kit>`__ beside your own.

Who to write to
---------------

* **Listing your club, new officers, events to announce, a support letter:** `studentclubs@neurotechx.org <mailto:studentclubs@neurotechx.org>`__
* **A Code of Conduct report:** `admins@neurotechx.org <mailto:admins@neurotechx.org>`__
* **Anything else:** `info@neurotechx.org <mailto:info@neurotechx.org>`__
